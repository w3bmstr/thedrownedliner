Story & how to play

THE STORY

R.M.S. Aurora sank in 1912 in under three hours. She rests at 3,784 metres. Sixteen relics remain in the dark — objects left by passengers and crew who never came home. Your dive is to find them all and bring their stories back to the light.

YOUR MISSION

Explore the wreck from bow to stern. Enter rooms to learn what happened there. Collect all 16 relics. Each one has a short history when you recover it.

WHERE TO LOOK

  • Bow & bridge decks — officers, wireless, cabins
  • Grand staircase & public rooms — first-class spaces
  • Midships dining & hospital
  • Engine, boilers & cargo holds below
  • Stern — docking bridge, poop deck, steering gear, crew quarters

CONTROLS

Desktop: WASD swim · mouse look · Q/E or Space/Ctrl rise & dive · Shift boost · F sonar · L light · P photo · O menu

Phone: left stick move · right stick look · ▲▼ rise/dive · ⚡ boost · SONAR button · ☰ menu

TIPS

  • Use sonar (F or SONAR) to find the nearest relic and its direction
  • Assist sonar in the menu names the relic for you
  • Extra light helps in deep corridors
  • Minimap shows the ship layout and unfound relics
  • Save progress from the menu if you need to surface mid-dive
  • Dive journal (J or menu) logs rooms and relics you find
  • Light battery drains while the lamp is on — toggle it off in the menu, or stay still with light off to recharge
